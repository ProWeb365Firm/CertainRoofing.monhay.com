About Elias Marquez

Elias Marquez didn’t start Certain Roofing as a first step into the industry. He founded it on more than 25 years of family experience building, repairing, and replacing roofs and siding throughout the Twin Cities—in real Minnesota weather, on real homes. Elias created Certain Roofing because homeowners deserve to work directly with someone who understands how to get the job done right—not simply a sales representative or a company whose standards change depending on who wins the bid.

That’s how Certain Roofing got its name. When a company puts ‘certain’ in its name, it’s making a promise: certain materials, certain workmanship, and a certain answer when you ask a question. No hedging, no vague timelines, no surprises at the final invoice.

What We Believe

Honesty over upselling.

If your roof needs a repair, we tell you it needs a repair, not a full replacement to pad the invoice.

Direct accountability.

Elias is personally involved in estimates and jobs, not just answering the phone. When you have a question, you’re talking to the person who actually knows the answer.

Built for Minnesota, not the brochure.

Every material and method we use is chosen because it holds up to ice dams, hail, and freeze-thaw cycles, not because it looked good in a catalog.

One contractor, every season.

Roofing and siding slow down in the winter. We don’t. Interior remodeling and home improvement keep our crew, and your home projects, moving all year long.

Doing it by the book.

Minnesota requires licensed contractors to pull permits on most reroofing, siding, and larger remodeling work. We handle that paperwork as a matter of course, because skipped permits can cost homeowners their insurance coverage and resale value down the road.

Relationships over transactions.

We’re not looking to complete one project and move on. Our goal is to become the contractor you feel comfortable calling for years to come. That means earning your trust through honest advice and standing behind what we do long after the project is finished.

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Roofing Company

1. What experience did Elias bring to Certain Roofing?

Elias founded Certain Roofing on more than 25 years of family experience in the roofing and remodeling industry. That foundation includes hands-on work building, repairing, and replacing roofs and siding throughout the Twin Cities, providing practical knowledge of Minnesota’s demanding weather conditions. This trade-focused background is why Certain Roofing prioritizes honest technical assessments and appropriate solutions over sales-driven upselling.

2. What does "certain" actually mean in terms of how you price and quote projects?

A commitment to certainty in pricing means the estimate you receive reflects the real scope of the job rather than a low initial number designed to be inflated later with change orders. Homeowners should expect a detailed, itemized quote upfront rather than vague ranges that leave room for surprises at final invoice.

3. How does Certain Roofing stay current on Minnesota building codes and industry best practices?

Ongoing trade experience combined with the permit process itself, since every reroofing or siding project reviewed by a local building department, keeps a contractor current on code requirements as they evolve. Contractors who personally manage permitting on every eligible project, rather than skipping it, are also the ones most likely to stay current on code changes because they interact with inspectors directly.

4. What happens if a problem is discovered with my roof or siding after Elias has completed the project?

A workmanship-focused company built on long-term relationships rather than one-time transactions typically stands behind completed work with a warranty period and remains reachable for follow-up concerns rather than disappearing after the final payment. This is part of why the company positions itself as a contractor homeowners can call for the life of their house, not just a single job.

5. Does Certain Roofing participate in the local St. Paul or Woodbury community beyond roofing projects?

Locally owned, owner-operated businesses in Minnesota often build reputation and referral business through community visibility, such as sponsoring local events or working with area real estate agents and home inspectors. It’s reasonable to ask a contractor directly about local involvement if community reputation and neighborhood referrals matter to your decision.

6. What sets a company named around trustworthiness apart when every contractor claims to be honest?

The practical difference typically shows up in specifics: a company that documents its licensing and insurance clearly, has an identifiable owner personally involved in projects, and offers concrete, verifiable answers to direct questions distinguishes itself from vague marketing claims. Asking pointed questions about permits, warranty terms, and who will actually be on the job site is one of the most reliable ways to separate substance from marketing language.

7. How does the company balance being a small, locally owned business with having enough crew capacity for larger projects?

A local, owner-operated model can still maintain sufficient crew capacity for larger roofing, siding, or remodeling jobs by keeping a trained core team busy year-round through the mix of seasonal exterior work and winter interior remodeling. This staffing approach avoids the common small-contractor problem of losing skilled crew members during slow seasons.
