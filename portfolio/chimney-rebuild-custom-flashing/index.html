  • 01 ━ Before the rebuild

The Problem

The existing chimney had deteriorated and was allowing water to enter where it connected with the roof. The ongoing leaks caused damage inside the home, creating the risk of further deterioration to the surrounding roofing materials and structure. Previous repairs were not helpful, making a complete chimney rebuild the most dependable solution.

The dependable fix meant addressing the chimney and the roof connection together.

Existing condition

Reconstruction in progress

  • 02 ━ Built for the roofline

The Solution

Our team carefully removed the existing chimney, inspected the surrounding roof area, and prepared for reconstruction. We then built and installed a new custom chimney system designed specifically for the property. Custom flashing was fabricated and implemented around the chimney, along with the necessary surrounding roofing materials to create a durable and watertight connection between the two.

  • Existing chimney removed
  • Custom chimney rebuilt
  • Flashing fabricated on site
  • Watertight roof connection
  • 03 ━ A dependable finish

The Result

The newly constructed chimney and custom flashing corrected the source of the leaks and protected the home from continued interior water damage. This finished system provides improved durability, proper water management, and a clean appearance that integrates naturally with the roof. The homeowner now has peace of mind knowing the problem was addressed with a dependable long-term solution rather than another temporary repair.

A dependable long-term solution, not another temporary repair.
