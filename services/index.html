Roofing, Siding, and Home Improvement, Done Right, All Year.

Certain Roofing offers full exterior and interior services for homeowners across Saint Paul, Woodbury, and the Surrounding Metro. Every project is handled with the same standard: honest pricing, quality materials, and 25+ years of hands-on trade experience behind every estimate.

Roofing

Your roof is your home’s first line of defense against Minnesota’s ice, hail, wind, and snow load, and it’s the service Certain Roofing was built on.

Roof Replacement

Full tear-offs and re-roofs using durable, weather-rated materials installed by a crew that’s done this thousands of times. Most residential asphalt shingle roofs in Minnesota last 20–30 years depending on ventilation, installation quality, and storm exposure; when yours is nearing the end of its service life or has sustained storm damage, a full replacement is the more cost-effective long-term choice over repeated repairs.

Roof Repair

Leaks, missing shingles, flashing issues, and everything in between, fixed without being upsold into a replacement you don’t need.

Roof Inspections

A thorough, honest look at your roof’s condition, with clear photos and a plain-English explanation of what you’re looking at, including attic ventilation and insulation, since poor attic conditions are the leading cause of ice dams and premature shingle failure in Minnesota.

Storm Damage & Insurance Claims

Hail and wind damage documentation and support navigating the insurance claims process, including meeting your adjuster on-site, so the settlement reflects the real scope of damage, not just what’s visible from the street.

Metal & Specialty Roofing

We install standing-seam metal, cedar shake, or other specialty systems in addition to asphalt shingles.

Permits

Minnesota’s State Building Code (Minnesota Rules Chapter 1309) requires a permit for most residential reroofing projects. Permit approval typically takes one to two weeks depending on the city. As the contractor of record, Certain Roofing pulls and manages this permit on every eligible project, so your work stays code-compliant and your insurance coverage isn’t put at risk by unpermitted work.

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Roofing Services

1. How do I know if my roof needs a repair or a full replacement?

A roof past 20 years old, or one showing widespread granule loss, curling shingles, or damage across multiple sections rather than one isolated spot, usually costs more to keep patching than to replace outright. A thorough inspection that checks attic ventilation and insulation alongside the shingles themselves gives the clearest picture of whether a repair will actually hold or just delay the inevitable.

2. What should I expect during the insurance adjuster meeting for a storm damage claim?

The adjuster will walk the roof and document visible hail or wind damage, and having your contractor present during that walk-through helps ensure damage that isn’t obvious from the ground, like bruised shingles or compromised flashing, gets included in the settlement. Contractors experienced in storm claims can also point out related damage, such as gutters or siding, that homeowners might not think to mention.

3. How is standing-seam metal roofing different from asphalt shingles in terms of performance and cost?

Standing-seam metal roofing typically costs two to three times more upfront than asphalt shingles but can last 40-70 years compared to 20-30 years for shingles, and it sheds snow and ice more efficiently, which reduces ice-dam risk in Minnesota winters. The higher initial investment is usually justified for homeowners planning to stay long-term or seeking to minimize maintenance over decades.

4. What's the difference between vinyl, fiber cement, and engineered wood siding beyond cost, in terms of maintenance?

Vinyl requires the least ongoing maintenance since it never needs repainting, while fiber cement and engineered wood (like LP SmartSide) need periodic caulking and repainting touch-ups roughly every five to seven years to maintain their weather resistance. Fiber cement generally holds up best against hail and wind impact of the three, which is a meaningful factor in Minnesota’s storm-prone climate.

5. How does basement finishing or bathroom remodeling get scheduled around your roofing and siding season?

Interior projects like basement finishing and bathroom remodels are scheduled year-round and specifically fill the winter months when exterior roofing and siding work pauses for weather, giving a contractor consistent crew availability regardless of season. This means a homeowner planning both an exterior project and an interior remodel can often coordinate both with the same contractor without seasonal conflicts.

6. Do you offer color and material consultations before I commit to a siding choice?

Color and material consultations focus on how a given siding option will actually perform against Minnesota’s freeze-thaw cycles and sun exposure, not just how it looks in a showroom sample, which helps avoid choosing a material that looks great initially but underperforms over the following decade. This consultation typically happens during the estimate phase, before any contract is signed.

7. What Minnesota building code requirements apply to interior remodeling projects like bathrooms or basements?

Interior remodels involving plumbing, electrical, or structural changes typically fall under the Minnesota State Building Code and require permits and inspections, similar to exterior reroofing projects. A contractor who handles both exterior and interior work under one roof, including permit management, removes the burden of coordinating separate permitting processes for different trades.
