Our services

Our Home Remodeling Services

We offer kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, basement finishing, and general home improvement. Interior work is scheduled year-round, including winter. Discuss the specific work you need during your estimate.

Kitchen Remodeling

The kitchen is the heart of the home and one of the best places to invest. We update cabinets, counters, lighting, flooring, and layouts. Learn more on our kitchen remodeling page.

Bathroom Remodeling

We turn cramped, dated bathrooms into clean, bright spaces. That can mean a new tub or walk-in shower, a new vanity, tile floors, better lighting, and a fan that actually clears the steam. We also make bathrooms safer with grab bars and low or no-step showers.

Basement Finishing

A finished basement adds living space without adding on to your home. We can create a family room, a guest room, a home office, or a play area. In Minnesota, basements need extra care. We check for moisture first and use materials that handle cool, damp conditions.

Flooring

New floors change the look of a whole room. We install luxury vinyl plank, tile, and other durable options. We fix uneven subfloors first, so your new floor stays flat and quiet.

Drywall, Trim, and Paint

Cracked walls and worn trim make a home look tired. We repair drywall and plaster, install new baseboards and door trim, and paint for a fresh, finished look.

Opening Up Your Floor Plan

Many older homes have small, closed-off rooms. Taking out a wall can bring in light and make your home feel bigger. Some walls hold up your house, so we check first and bring in an engineer when needed. We also get the right permits so the work is safe and up to code.

Stay longer

Updates That Help You Stay in Your Home Longer

Many homeowners want to stay in the home they love as they get older. A few smart changes make that much easier and safer:

  • Walk-in or curbless showers with grab bars and a built-in seat
  • Comfort-height toilets and lever-style door handles
  • Wider doorways and hallways for walkers or wheelchairs
  • Brighter lighting on stairs and in hallways
  • Slip-resistant flooring in kitchens and bathrooms
  • A main-floor bedroom or bathroom, when space allows

These updates also make life easier for young families and guests. We can plan them as part of a bigger remodel or as small projects on their own.

Energy-Saving Upgrades Inside Your Home

Minnesota winters are long, and heating bills add up. Some interior projects help your home stay warmer for less. Sealing air leaks around outlets, trim, and attic hatches stops drafts. Adding insulation to basement walls makes lower levels more comfortable. LED lighting cuts electric use. If your attic needs more insulation, our roofing team can check that too.

Older homes

Remodeling Older St. Paul Homes

St. Paul is full of beautiful older homes with real character. They also come with real challenges. Plaster walls crack. Old wiring may not handle today’s needs. Floors may sag, and walls are rarely square.

We know how to work with older homes. We keep the charm you love, like wood trim and built-ins, while making the home safer and easier to live in.

Homes built before 1978

Homes built before 1978 may have lead paint. Before work that could disturb painted surfaces, ask about applicable lead-safe requirements and request proof of firm certification and worker qualifications. Certain Roofing's EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm status has not yet been confirmed.

Planning

How to Plan Your Home Remodel

A little planning makes any remodel go smoother. Here is where we suggest you start:

  1. List what bugs you. Not enough storage? Too dark? Not enough outlets? Problems guide the plan.
  2. Set a budget range. You do not need an exact number, but a range helps us suggest the right options.
  3. Plan a cushion. Set aside about 10 to 15 percent extra for surprises, like hidden water damage or old wiring.
  4. Collect ideas. Save photos of rooms you like. They help us understand your style fast.
  5. Think about timing. Plan around holidays, school breaks, and guests.
  6. Get a free estimate. We will visit, listen, measure, and give you a clear written plan.

Home Remodeling FAQ

Still wondering about something? Call (651) 925-9293 and ask.

How much does a home remodel cost in St. Paul?

It depends on the rooms, the size of the space, and the materials you choose. A new floor and fresh paint cost much less than a full bathroom or basement remodel. We give you a free written estimate with clear choices, so you can decide what fits your budget.

How long will my remodel take?

Timing depends on the rooms involved, scope, permits, and materials. Ask for a project-specific schedule before work begins.

Do I need a permit for my remodel?

If your project includes new wiring, plumbing, or moving walls, you will likely need a city permit. Simple updates like paint or new flooring usually do not. We know the rules in St. Paul and nearby cities, and we handle permits for you.

Can I live at home during the remodel?

Yes, in most cases. We seal off the work area with plastic walls to control dust and clean up at the end of each day. If we are working on your only bathroom, we plan the schedule to keep downtime as short as possible.

What is interior home improvement?

Interior home improvement means any project that makes the inside of your home better. That includes new floors, fresh paint, updated kitchens and baths, finished basements, and better lighting. These projects can add comfort, save energy, and raise your home’s value.

Which remodeling projects add the most value?

Kitchens and bathrooms usually give the best return. Fresh paint and new flooring are low-cost ways to make a home feel new. A finished basement adds living space. We help you focus on projects that fit your goals, whether you plan to stay or sell.

Do you work on older homes?

Discuss your home’s age and condition during your estimate. For homes built before 1978, resolve lead-paint requirements and verify contractor qualifications before work that could disturb painted surfaces.

How do I get ready for my remodel?

Clear out the room we will be working in, along with nearby shelves and walls. Put away fragile items in the rooms next to it. Plan where pets will stay during work hours. We will walk you through a simple checklist before day one.

Can you help me pick finishes and colors?

Yes. We bring samples of flooring, tile, paint, and fixtures, and we share what has worked well in other homes. We will help you choose finishes that fit your style, your budget, and how hard you are on your home.

Why hire one general contractor instead of separate trades?

A general contractor can provide one point of contact for scheduling, materials, and permits. Ask which trades are included in your estimate and who will perform any electrical and plumbing work.
