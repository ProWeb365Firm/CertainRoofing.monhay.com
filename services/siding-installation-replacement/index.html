Warning signs

Signs It Is Time for New Siding

Siding does not last forever. Here are the most common signs that yours needs help:

Cracks, holes, or warped boards

These let water get behind the siding.

Loose or missing pieces

Strong Minnesota winds can pull weak siding right off the wall.

Faded or chalky color

Sun and weather break down the surface over time.

Dents or holes from hail

Even small dents can crack in the cold.

Mold, mildew, or soft spots

These can mean water is trapped behind the wall.

Higher heating bills or drafts

Old siding with poor wrap underneath can let cold air in.

If you see one or two of these in a small area, a repair may be enough. If they show up all over the house, it is usually time for siding replacement.

Materials

Choosing the Right Siding for Minnesota Weather

Our winters are cold and long. Our summers bring heat, humidity, and hail. The right siding needs to handle all of it. We offer vinyl, fiber cement including James Hardie, and engineered wood including LP SmartSide. We will help you pick the best fit for your home and budget.

Vinyl Siding

Vinyl is the most popular choice for many homeowners. It costs the least up front and never needs paint. It comes in many colors and styles. Insulated vinyl adds a foam backing that helps keep your home warmer.

Fiber Cement Siding

Fiber cement is made from cement, sand, and wood fibers. It is very strong, it will not burn, and bugs will not eat it. It looks like real painted wood. Brands like James Hardie are well known. Fiber cement costs more than vinyl and is heavier to install. It needs fresh paint about every 10 to 15 years, but it can last a very long time.

Engineered Wood Siding

Engineered wood, such as LP SmartSide, is made from wood strands bonded with special resins. It gives you the warm look of real wood, but it is treated to resist moisture, rot, and termites. It is lighter than fiber cement and stands up well to hail. It needs paint over time, just like fiber cement.

Not sure which is right for you? We will bring samples to your home so you can see and feel each option in real light.

Behind the siding

What Is Behind Your Siding Matters

The siding you see is only part of the story. What goes behind it keeps your walls dry for years. When we install new siding, we:

  • Check the wood sheathing and replace any rot
  • Install house wrap, a weather barrier that keeps water out but lets moisture escape
  • Add flashing around windows and doors, where most leaks start
  • Leave the right gaps so siding can expand and shrink with the seasons
  • Finish with new trim, soffit, and fascia so the whole house looks sharp

Skipping these steps is how cheap siding jobs fail. We never skip them.

Soffit, Fascia, and Trim

Siding is not the only part of your home’s outside that wears out. Soffit is the panel under the edge of your roof. Fascia is the board that runs along the roof edge and holds your gutters. Trim frames your windows, doors, and corners.

When these parts rot or crack, water and pests can get into your attic and walls. Vented soffit also helps your attic breathe, which keeps your roof healthy and helps prevent ice dams. We can replace soffit, fascia, and trim as part of your siding project, or on their own. Low-upkeep materials like aluminum and vinyl mean less painting for years to come.

Siding FAQ

Still wondering about something? Call (651) 925-9293 and ask.

How much does new siding cost?

The price depends on the size of your home, the type of siding, and how much repair is needed underneath. Vinyl costs the least. Fiber cement and engineered wood cost more but last a long time. We give you a free written estimate with clear options, so you can compare before you decide.

What is the best siding for Minnesota winters?

Vinyl, fiber cement, and engineered wood can work well here when installed correctly. The best choice depends on your budget, your home’s style, and how much upkeep you want. Ask which products are available for your project; insulated vinyl availability is not yet confirmed.

How long does siding last?

Vinyl siding often lasts 20 to 40 years. Fiber cement and engineered wood can last even longer with fresh paint over time. Good house wrap and flashing behind the siding help any type last longer.

Can you replace just one side of my house?

Yes. If only one wall was damaged, we can replace just that area. The hard part is matching color, since older siding fades. We will show you how close the match is before we start, so there are no surprises.

Can you install siding in winter?

Siding work depends on weather and product installation requirements. Call to discuss current scheduling for your home.

Will new siding lower my energy bills?

It can help. New house wrap seals out drafts, and insulated vinyl adds a layer of foam. The biggest savings usually come from attic insulation and sealing air leaks, but new siding with a good weather barrier is part of a warmer home.

Does insurance pay for siding damaged by hail?

Often, yes. Most homeowners policies cover sudden damage from hail or wind. We can inspect your siding for free and give you photos to share with your insurance company.

How do I choose a siding color?

Start with what will not change, like your roof, brick, or stone. Pick a siding color that works with those. Look at homes you like in your neighborhood, and check your HOA rules if you have them. We bring large samples so you can see colors on your actual house in daylight.

Do I need to be home during the project?

No. Many homeowners go to work as usual. We just need access to the outside of the house and an outlet for our tools. We will check in with you each day so you always know where things stand.
